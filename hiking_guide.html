🎒 What to Carry While Hiking

Essentials

Always Bring These

  • 💧 Water (more than you think you'll need)
  • 🍫 Snacks or energy bars
  • 🗺️ Trail map or GPS app
  • 📱 Fully charged phone
  • 🔋 Portable charger / power bank
  • 🩹 First aid kit
  • 🔦 Flashlight or headlamp
  • 🔧 Multi-tool or pocket knife
  • 🧴 Sunscreen
  • 🦟 Bug spray
  • 🕶️ Sunglasses
  • 🧢 Hat or cap
  • 📯 Emergency whistle
  • 🧥 Rain jacket or poncho
  • 🧦 Extra socks
  • 🎒 Small backpack
For Longer Hikes

Extended Trail Gear

  • 🥾 Trekking poles
  • 💧 Water filter or purification tablets
  • 🧭 Compass
  • 🔥 Fire starter or waterproof matches
  • 🏕️ Emergency blanket
  • 🏕️ Lightweight towel
  • 👕 Extra layers
  • ⛺ Camping gear if overnight
Quick Tips

Packing Smart

  • ✅ Pack your bag the night before so you don't forget anything.
  • ✅ Bring at least 0.5L of water per hour of hiking.
  • ✅ Keep your first aid kit at the top of your bag.
  • ✅ Snacks should be calorie-dense and easy to eat on the move.
  • ✅ Always carry a physical map — phone batteries die.

👕 What to Wear While Hiking

Clothing

Layer Up Right

  • 👕 Moisture-wicking shirt
  • 👖 Hiking pants or athletic shorts
  • 🩲 Breathable underwear
  • 🧦 Hiking socks (wool or synthetic)
  • 🧥 Lightweight fleece or jacket
  • 🌧️ Waterproof outer layer if rain is possible
Footwear

Protect Your Feet

  • 🥾 Hiking boots for rough terrain
  • 👟 Trail runners for easier trails
  • 👞 Shoes with good grip and ankle support

👟 Pro Tip

Break in new boots before a long hike — blisters from stiff new shoes are one of the most common trail complaints.

❄️ Cold Weather Gear

  • 🧥 Thermal layers
  • 🧤 Gloves
  • 🎿 Beanie
  • 🧥 Insulated jacket

☀️ Hot Weather Gear

  • 👕 Lightweight breathable clothing
  • 🎩 Sun hat
  • 🧊 Cooling towel

👁️ What to Look Out For

⚠️ Trail Safety

Hazards on the Trail

  • Loose rocks and slippery roots
  • Muddy sections
  • Sudden weather changes
  • Trail markers / signs
  • Steep drop-offs
  • River crossings
🐾 Wildlife & Nature

What You May Encounter

  • 🐍 Snakes
  • 🐻 Bears
  • 🕷️ Ticks
  • 🦟 Mosquitoes
  • 🌿 Poison ivy or poisonous plants
➕ Health & Safety

Health Risks

  • Dehydration
  • Heat exhaustion
  • Hypothermia
  • Blisters
  • Fatigue
✔ Good Hiking Habits
  • Stay on marked trails
  • Tell someone where you're going
  • Check weather before leaving
  • Start early enough to finish before dark
  • Pack out all trash
  • Respect wildlife and other hikers

🌲 Popular East Coast Hiking Areas

📍 Georgia to Maine

Appalachian Trail

A 2,190-mile trail stretching from Georgia to Maine, offering diverse landscapes and unforgettable views.

📍 Virginia

Shenandoah National Park

Over 500 miles of trails through the Blue Ridge Mountains with stunning overlooks and waterfalls.

📍 Maine

Acadia National Park

Hike mountains, forests, and coastal trails with breathtaking ocean views in Maine.

📍 New Hampshire

White Mountain National Forest

Home to rugged peaks, scenic valleys, and some of the best hiking in New England.

📍 TN & NC

Great Smoky Mountains National Park

Explore lush forests, historic structures, and incredible biodiversity.
