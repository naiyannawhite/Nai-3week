352 Mountain Road, Underhill, VT 05481

Trail Information

Distance: 8.8 Miles, Rount Trip

Elevation Gain: 2, 700 ft

Difficulty: Very Difficult

Parking: At Underhill State Park, on the Westside. Free across from the Barnes Camp Visitor Center, but that's on the opposite side of the mountain.

Enterance Fees: $5.00 for Adults (14+), $2.00 for Children (4-13), Free for Small Children (0-3)

Steepness: Very Steep

Time: 4-6 hours

Opening Hours: Open 24/7 when it's not closed for maintenance or weather conditions.

Peak Hiking Season: Later Spring - Fall

Fees and Passes for All Vermont State Parks

Arrive before 8:00 AM to avoid crowds, especially on weekends.

Restrooms: None avaliable on this trail

Important: Trail is closed from Mid March to Memorial Day Weekend because of the mud season

Additional Information: Poor Cell Coverage and Few Parking Options

Warning: DO NOT ATTEMPT TO HIKE IF THERE'S A THREAT OF BAD WEATHER OR LACK OF SUPPLIES

Tips and Tricks from Vermont's Green Mountain Club
